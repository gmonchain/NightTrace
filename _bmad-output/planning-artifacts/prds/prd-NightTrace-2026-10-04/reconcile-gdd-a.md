# Reconciliation Audit — `01-product-and-game-design.md` (lines 1–1400)

**Method.** Lines 1–1400 of the INPUT only (Read with offset/limit, 400-line chunks). Cross-read against `prd.md` and `addendum.md` in full. An item is a *gap* when it appears in the INPUT and appears in neither the PRD nor the addendum, in no FR, and in no non-goal. An item is a *contradiction* when the PRD/addendum asserts something the INPUT contradicts and no `[OVERRIDE]` tag names that change.

**Deliberately excluded** (per instructions, already known): (a) the Case Report's retained percentages — documented at PRD OQ-1, A-2, §4.6 `[OVERRIDE]`, addendum B.6; (b) monetization deferred to free at the owner's instruction — documented at PRD §5, §6.2, addendum A.1 row 13.

**Severity scale.** Critical = kills a load-bearing fictional or tonal mechanism. High = a real product behaviour is lost or silently reversed. Medium = a concrete decision is lost or an exact figure/vocabulary drifted. Low = craft detail, copy, or tuning.

## 4. Prose — the qualitative and tonal losses

The PRD is a good requirements document and a poor replacement for §B.7, §F.5, §F.9, and §F.10 of the INPUT. The losses cluster in one place: **the product's negative space.** The PRD's FRs are almost entirely about what the app *does* when something happens. The INPUT spends its most careful prose on what the app says when nothing does, and most of that prose does not survive the translation.

**Silence, narrated.** INPUT §A.5 lists "Silence, narrated" as the product's signature move and its biggest differentiator. The PRD keeps the *mechanism* (FR-2's silence floors, the memoryless draw) and the *state words* (`QUIET → LISTENING → ACTIVE → CONTACT`), but it drops the four named no-event outcomes that give silence a face (G-1) and reduces the negative-space panel — a generated, sensor-gated, four-line maximum block of absence copy — to a single sentence (G-2). The INPUT's most sophisticated moment, `WINDOW_CLOSED_EMPTY`, whose whole design is that the user *feels* the window and is *told nothing*, has no PRD presence at all. Downstream, the report's negative-space panel and the Case Report's "Nothing was recorded tonight. That is a result." line will be built with no spec for what else belongs there, and the sensor-gated omission rule — the honesty line that kept the panel from claiming "no electromagnetic activity" on a phone with no magnetometer — is unstated anywhere.

**Hedging vocabulary and the writing rules.** These survive, and it is worth being explicit so nobody "fixes" them: addendum I.1 carries *never assert / never wink / never explain the mechanic*, the hedge set (`Possible`, `unconfirmed`, `unsigned`, `no match on file`, `the record is unclear`), the **maximum nine words on any in-session line**, and *"the word 'ghost' appears only in the Hunt name"*. What is lost is the enumeration behind rule 3: the INPUT says the user is never told about *phases, tension, budgets, or rarity — ever* (L201–203). The PRD's OQ-5 then reopens the directive question as unresolved, and FR-6 tells the reader, in normative requirement text, what each archetype's verb and fail state is — fine for the builder, but the *enforcement* rule that none of this may reach the user is now only a half-sentence in a non-normative addendum rather than a testable constraint.

**The failure experience.** INPUT §A.2.7's whole point is that failure is where the genre turns hostile, and that NightTrace designed four unique fail states that each produce *a better story*. The PRD keeps the fail-state names as glossary vocabulary and as FR-6 consequences; it drops the six named fail-state copy strings (G-10), the five named report stamps and diegetic badges (G-9), and the `MISDIRECTED` rule that the app stays silent in-session and only confesses in the report revision line. `MISDIRECTED` in particular is now unreachable: its trigger requires the delayed cross-tool Voice answer that FR-13 deletes (C-2).

**Tonal-craft material with no home.** A cluster of small, high-quality decisions are absent from both PRD and addendum and would be lost to a new builder: the dry log's *"You marked a spot with no reading. That is also a record."* (G-11); the ambience bed that gets *quieter and duller* as tension rises rather than louder (G-17); the one-haptic-per-800 ms limit; pull-to-refresh being disabled so a spinner never breaks the illusion (G-19); capture storing the photo with the overlay composited out because compositing would be a claim (G-15); and calibration's `quiet` / `noisy surroundings` symmetry with the note *"Neither is a judgment; both are true and both are used."* (G-6). None is load-bearing alone; together they are most of what makes the app read as an instrument rather than a toy.

**Two structural absences.** The Directives system (G-3) and the power contract (G-4) are whole features in the INPUT — one governs every line of in-session guidance, the other is the mitigation for a top-five risk — and neither has an FR. The Directive system degraded into an open question; the power contract degraded into one NFR sentence and a known open number.

**A note on the `[OVERRIDE]` regime.** The PRD's override discipline is real and mostly good — fourteen owner-brief overrides are tagged and ratified. But every untagged change in §1 above is a change against the *brainstorm*, not the owner's brief, and the PRD's framing ("This PRD supersedes and consolidates four inputs… Where the sources disagree, this PRD is authoritative") makes that legitimate in principle while leaving it invisible in practice. C-1 and C-2 are the ones that matter: both silently reverse fictional mechanisms the surrounding FRs still depend on, and a downstream builder reading only the PRD will implement a Field Note with a report (or no knowledge that it was ever intended) and a Voice tool whose answers arrive in 300 ms, then wonder why the Mimic archetype has nothing to mimic.
