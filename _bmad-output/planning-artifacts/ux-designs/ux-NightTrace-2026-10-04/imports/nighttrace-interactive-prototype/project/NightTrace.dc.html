INVESTIGATION, NOT DETECTION

The vocabulary is borrowed from paperwork: ruled hairlines, ledger rows, a rubber stamp, a typed footer. Instruments draw shapes — traces, cones, ladders, arcs — but never a scale, an axis or a needle. A shape can be read; it cannot be checked.

WHY SAFELIGHT RED

Darkrooms and astronomers use deep red because it spares dark-adapted eyes. It is the only chromatic colour, and it is reserved for ink: the seal, the REC dot, lit signature slots, the hold fill, the one badge. Everything else is bone on warm black.

WHERE I HELD BACK

No glow, no scan lines, no shadows, no gradients beyond the vignette. The radar sweep turns once a minute. Encounter frames sit at low opacity, off-centre, blurred. Nothing moves faster than breathing except a haptic.

DEVIATIONS FROM THE BRIEF

The INTERFERENCE report stamp read “…DETECTED” — a banned term — so it ends at MAGNETIC INTERFERENCE. Voice and Sky readouts use letter bands and compass words instead of numbers. At large type sizes the tool row becomes a two-row labelled grid rather than shrinking.
