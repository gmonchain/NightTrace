First principles · bedrock

Primary constraint

Uncertainty is the product. Predictability is the anti-metric.

Every system is judged by one question: does it protect uncertainty or spend it? If a user can predict when an event fires, the product is dead. This single law produced the silence mandate, the no-encounter protection, and the ban on verifiable numbers — they are all the same decision wearing different clothes.

Field notes · curated by theme

A

The reframe

First Principles
  • The phone is a case file that writes itself — the device narrates, it does not measure.
  • Change the verb from detect to investigate. Detection implies proof; investigation implies process.
  • Users cannot verify any claim, so the only real deliverable is a story they believe they took part in.
  • Sensors are inherently noisy — treat the noise as content, not an obstacle to filter.
  • A session is a field trip, not a tool launch.
  • Evidence is not data — it is a souvenir of a moment.
  • Never show a number a user could try to verify.
B

The jobs

JTBD
  • Give me a reason to feel something on a dull night walk — an ambient companion.
  • Hand me a story to tell my friends tomorrow.
  • Let me play pretend seriously for twenty minutes without winking at me.
  • Make my ordinary place feel mysterious.
  • Scare me safely, on my own terms.
  • Give me something to do with my kids or on a Friday date.
  • Biggest job: convert the mundane — commute, attic, backyard — into the meaningful.
  • Anti-jobs: they are not hiring it to learn science, to truly detect ghosts, or to be deceived.
C

Absence & pacing

Reverse + Dial 11
  • No-encounter sessions are valid — even good. Protect them in the design.
  • Mandate long silences between signals; an event every 10 seconds is the failure mode.
  • The scare is almost, peripheral, ambiguous. The scare is a maybe.
  • A dead-quiet hunt whose entire reward is one impossible sound after 15 minutes.
  • Silence plus ambience — music only ever as an encounter sting.
  • Every target gets a birth, a velocity, an uncertainty, and a death. No forever-random dots.
  • Assign micro-jobs: ask a question, sweep a baseline, log a spot.
D

Evidence & the receipt

Report-first
  • Evidence compounds toward a creature signature — never feels random.
  • Triage + trickster false evidence + end-of-case rating = a deduction mini-layer that makes the report feel earned.
  • The report is the job's receipt: proof-of-experience, not proof-of-ghost.
  • Treat the report as the hero screen, not an afterthought — build it before the tools.
  • Auto-assemble the strongest evidence into an evidence-reel share video.
  • Native, watermark-free, beautiful share card. If a moment can't be screenshotted, it didn't happen.
E

Infinite variety

Seeding
  • Seed from place + hour + weather + sensor noise = a one-time, unrepeatable fingerprint.
  • Daily briefing: “the anomaly tonight is…” — different every day.
  • Shared-seed nights: everyone worldwide hunts the same generated anomaly.
  • Personal → daily → global is one idea at three altitudes: a rotating world clock that manufactures variety for free.
  • Real weather API drives event tables — a thunderstorm unlocks a storm entity.
  • Legendary entity as a 0.5% event, stated plainly: no one in your area has seen it.
  • GPS + dusk + weather declare a real hot zone: tonight, your area is active.
F

Ritual & anticipation

Ritual Innovation
  • Short gear-up checklist every hunt: calibrate, name the case, set intention.
  • Ask the dark a question aloud — and the payoff may arrive minutes later.
  • Naming your investigation turns a session into a possession.
  • Design the threshold crossing: from calibrated and ready into the dark.
  • Debrief prompt — what did you hear? — inviting the user to write their own account.
  • Close the case with a stamped, ceremonially final report. A seal-the-evidence gesture locks the file.
  • Cold-start mode: no UI chrome at all. A dark screen and one button.
G

Money & trust

Worst Idea, flipped
  • Zero mid-session ads; monetize only at natural case boundaries.
  • Unlimited hunts — scarcity lives in rare creatures, not energy timers.
  • Fully deterministic unlocks with visible requirements. No loot boxes, ever.
  • Zero-login, offline-first; an account only to sync or restore.
  • Private journal by default; sharing is opt-in, per moment.
  • Earned dread with an explicit intensity slider.
  • Four deep creatures each with a unique verb and fail state — not forty reskins.
  • Never make a falsifiable claim, and never claim science. Frame the simulation openly.
H

Tech collisions

Emerging Tech
  • On-device audio classification maps real ambient sound — wind, a car — to in-game events. The room becomes the script.
  • Whisper into the mic; local keyword spotting makes the entity respond to what you said.
  • Director Mode async: a friend replays your case and injects events into your next session.
  • Wearable heart-rate as a tension dial — real fear makes the entity escalate.
  • Two phones synchronised into one shared case, in real time. Local co-op, no server.
  • Case trade: gift a rare evidence card or creature intro to a friend.
  • Later: ARKit or LiDAR anchors apparitions in real 3D space.
I

Trust boundaries

Permissions
  • Ask for permissions just in time — never up front.
  • A ghost hunt must run on mic only; the torch, camera and location are opt-in layers.
  • Duty-cycle the sensors; offer a dimmed low-power mode so a long walk doesn't drain the phone.
  • No loading spinners — everything is local and instant.
  • The entity remembers you and references your last case across sessions.
  • If the user can decode the event engine, the magic dies — hold the mask.

07

Sealed verdict

Six decisions · closed 2026-10-04T11:12

Decisions of record

Do not reopen without new evidence

  1. Ship four creatures deep, not many shallow — each with a unique verb and a unique fail state. Scope frozen · future creatures parked as content drops

  2. Build the report and share card before the tools. The hero screen is the growth engine; everything else feeds it

  3. Use qualitative readouts everywhere; expose no verifiable numbers. Unexplained · Inconclusive · Explained replaces every score

  4. The free first hunt must include a real encounter; place the paywall after the second case. No ads mid-session — monetize only at case boundaries

  5. Data-driven creature definitions with behaviour archetypes; the engine never hardcodes a creature. Future creatures ship with zero engine work

  6. Four tabs only: Home · Investigate · Field Journal · Profile. The Equipment tab is cut — tools live inside hunts

Open anomalies & the loop forward

Unresolved on purpose

The growth flywheel

Tools generate the material, the report packages it, the share card recruits, and the new hunter generates more. The loop is closed by the souvenir, not by the sensor.

Risk register

  • HIGHThe skeptic's test. Any falsifiable claim kills retention and reviews inside the first 30 seconds of scrutiny.
  • HIGHPredictability. Once users decode the event engine, the magic dies and there is no second act.
  • MEDFeels fake when tested. Tension between "no winking" immersion and openly framing the simulation.
  • MEDReport quality. If the hero screen is weak or ugly, the entire flywheel stalls.
  • MEDUncertainty vs. reward. Too little happening reads as broken, not atmospheric.
  • MEDBattery. Always-on sensing against a product whose best sessions are long walks.
  • HIGHScope. Four deep creatures is far more work than forty shallow ones — and the whole bet rests on that.
